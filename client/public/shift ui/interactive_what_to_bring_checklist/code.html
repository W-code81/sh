Top 3 Forgotten Items

Every season, over 40% of attendees arrive without these vital essentials:

  • 1

    Flashlight or headlamp

    Mountain paths between firepit & cabins have no streetlights.

  • 2

    Warm extra wool socks

    Morning grass dew soaks shoes immediately; dry feet are key.

  • 3

    Reusable insulated water bottle

    High elevation dehydrates twice as quickly as sea level.
