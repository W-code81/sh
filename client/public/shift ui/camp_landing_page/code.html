Everything You Need To Know

Camp Guidelines & Essential Logistics

Safety, comfort, and peace of mind are top priorities. Have questions regarding cabin sorting, allergies, or drop-off timing? Read our core provisions.

support_agent
Have Special Medical Needs?

Our church camp safety directors can coordinate private accommodations for prescriptions or allergies.

Email Health Director arrow_outward

Campers stay in fully insulated, carpeted wooden mountain cabins equipped with dual air-conditioning/heating units, bunk beds, and electrical outlets for CPAP or essentials.

  • Strictly segregated male and female cabin zones supervised by vetted counselors.
  • Grouped by academic cohort: High School (9th–12th) and Young Adults (18–25).
  • Cabin buddy requests can be submitted during step 2 of registration checkout.

All 9 meals are cooked fresh daily by Pinecrest's culinary team. Diners enjoy hot entrees, comprehensive salad bar, soup kettles, and fresh fruit stations.

Gluten-free, vegetarian, dairy-free, and nut-safe menus are labeled clearly at every meal. Please note your dietary requirements on your registration intake form so allergen wristbands are pre-assigned.

Two transport options are available:

  • Church Bus Option: Departs Grace Community Church campus at 11:30 AM on Thursday, Aug 14 (free seat reservation included in registration).
  • Caravan / Self-Drive: Follow the group convoy departing at 12:00 PM or arrive directly at Pinecrest Grounds between 2:00 PM and 3:30 PM.

We uphold an unconditional commitment to safe youth environments:

  • Pinecrest Retreat features a dedicated on-site medical infirmary staffed around the clock by a registered pediatric RN.
  • All adult leaders and small-group directors are Livescan fingerprint checked, First-Aid/CPR certified, and MinistrySafe trained.
  • Zero-tolerance policy on alcohol, smoking, vaping, weapons, and leaving grounds without leadership authorization.
