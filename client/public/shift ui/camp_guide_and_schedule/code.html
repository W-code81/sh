Key Protocols

Camp Essentials & Guidelines

Important instructions for maintaining a safe, respectful, and spirit-filled community environment.

bedtime Quiet Hours & Curfew

Strict cabin curfew is 11:00 PM nightly. Quiet hours remain in effect until 06:45 AM. Campers may not leave assigned cabins after curfew without a counselor.

cleaning_services Clean Cabin Cup

Daily bunk inspections at 11:30 AM. Points awarded for made beds, swept floors, and proper gear stowage. Winning cabin receives ice cream party privileges!

devices Tech & Device Etiquette

Phones are permitted for camp app reference and photography only. Cabins participate in device-free reflection hours between 10:00 PM and 07:00 AM.

Standard Buffet Windows

Breakfast: 08:00 AM – 09:15 AM
Lunch: 12:30 PM – 01:45 PM
Dinner: 05:30 PM – 06:45 PM

Snack Shack & Coffee Corner

Open daily 02:00 PM – 04:30 PM and 09:30 PM – 10:30 PM. Camp lanyards can be preloaded via registration portal or paid with card.

* Dietary Notice: All meals clearly label Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, and Vegan options. Please inform server staff of severe anaphylactic allergies upon entry.

Pinecrest Waterfront & Boats

Certified lifeguards strictly enforce swim zones (01:30 PM – 05:00 PM). Life vests (PFDs) are mandatory for all paddleboards and canoes, regardless of swimming ability.

High Ropes & Zip Line Course

Requires 15-minute safety orientation. Closed-toe athletic shoes mandatory. Weight parameters: 65 lbs min, 250 lbs max per mountain harness specifications.

Emergency Muster Point: Main Soccer Field

In case of continuous siren horn, gather with cabin group at your numbered cabin flag pole.

Is there WiFi on campus?

Yes, high-speed Starlink WiFi network EventlyCamp_Guest covers the Main Lodge, Dining Hall, and Amphitheater. Cabins are intentionally low-signal zones.

What cell carriers work at Pinecrest?

Verizon and AT&T maintain 2-3 bars of LTE across the retreat ground. T-Mobile has coverage near the Main Lodge only.

What is the weather forecast?

Highs of 78°F during afternoon hours with crisp mountain breezes. Evenings dip into 50–54°F. A light rain jacket is recommended for afternoon mountain showers.

Lost and Found procedure

Items left at the Amphitheater or Dining Hall are brought to the Counselor Hospitality Station every evening. Label all water bottles and Bible cases!

Questions During Your Stay?
