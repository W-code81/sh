## Platform

## Users

Prospective campers and their families use the public site to understand the camp, register, and check their registration. The camp is being planned for approximately 50–90 attendees.

## Product Purpose

SHIFT Camp’s public website provides camp information and supports attendee registration and preparation.

## Positioning

No distinct market position has been established. Keep claims grounded in confirmed camp information.

## Operating Context

Visitors need to find camp dates, venue and lodging information, preparation guidance, and registration details. A separate admin app exists in the monorepo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The attendee app is a Next.js web application.
- Public routes cover camp information, registration, registration lookup, and preparation guidance.
- The user provided camp dates of December 17–20; the year currently used by the site is 2026.
- Attendance is an estimate of approximately 50–90 people, not a confirmed capacity.
- Do not present unconfirmed venue, price, schedule, capacity, staffing, or activity details as facts.

## Brand Commitments

- The camp is faith-based and run by a local church.
- The public camp brand is SHIFT Camp.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided camp photos are in `public/images/slideshow/`.
- The project includes a SHIFT Camp logo component.
- No testimonials, confirmed price, or full confirmed schedule are available in the current project context.

## Product Principles

- Make registration and practical preparation easy to find.
- Represent the faith and community purpose of the camp.
- Keep event information accurate and make undecided logistics clear.
